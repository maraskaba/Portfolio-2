Investments section redesign

UI design and front-end development to simplify a complex experience and improve understanding of investment products.

Client Zurich
Year 2020
Scope UI Design / Front-End

Context

Zurich offered a range of investment products within its digital ecosystem.

However, the section had comprehension problems, making it hard for users to understand the available alternatives and make informed decisions.

The main problem wasn't usability, but the lack of information needed to make decisions.

Goals

  • Improve understanding of investment products
  • Make it easier to compare options
  • Guide decision-making
  • Create a clearer, more trustworthy experience

My role

I joined the project once the initial research and a first architecture had already been defined.

My job was to take that foundation to a clear, usable, testable final experience, focusing on the interface, information hierarchy, and the functional prototype.

  • Evolving wireframes into high-fidelity visual design
  • Redesigning the experience around research insights
  • Improving hierarchy, clarity, and scannability of information
  • Incorporating a library that renders charts from JSON
  • Adapting the experience to mobile
  • Building a front-end prototype for validation

Proposal

The new experience reorganizes information around decision-making:

  • A clear view of the investment's current status
  • Visualization of evolution and performance
  • Comparison between key values
  • Access to detailed information without overloading the interface
  • Better navigation between sections

Validation with an HTML prototype

The final round of user testing was done with an HTML mockup, since the different features and the dynamic chart evaluation couldn't be done with traditional tools like InVision or Figma.

This made it possible to properly validate:

  • Understanding of charts
  • Interpretation of metrics
  • Navigation between sections
View prototype View prototype

Impact

The new design and user testing produced the following results:

  • All users found the information they were looking for
  • Significant improvement in data comprehension
  • High satisfaction compared to the previous version
  • Greater clarity when reading metrics
  • Increased confidence in making decisions
