Budgeted hours tracking & reconciliation

Design and validation of a dashboard to monitor budgeted hours, hours consumed, and financial deviations without relying on manual analysis.

Client Blink.la
Year 2024
Scope UX Design / UI Design / Exploratory Interviews

Context

Content Studio is a suite of tools designed to automate, control, and track audiovisual productions. The Finance team needed greater visibility into one of the projects' key data points. Data analysis relied on external tools.

Problem

Financial analysis depended on manual steps and external tools. Hours were logged in the time-tracking module, went through approvals, and then had to be reconciled against the corresponding budget.

To gain visibility, the team downloaded the data as a CSV, uploaded it to an external dashboard, and reviewed deviations in weekly meetings.

This created three main problems:

Fragmented information

The data needed to understand a production's financial status was spread across different modules, spreadsheets, and external dashboards.

Lack of early visibility

Deviations between logged hours and budget were detected late, during the Finance team's weekly review.

Manual operational process

The team relied on exports, data cross-checking, and meetings to understand which productions were within or outside budget.

Goals

  • Integrate the financial dashboard within Content Studio.
  • Enable early alerts through visual indicators and notifications.
  • Simplify the reconciliation of pending hours.
  • Improve the Finance team's efficiency in detecting and following up on issues.

Design approach

The analysis showed that the problem wasn't just about visualizing data, but about connecting different parts of the flow: hour logging, approval, budgeting, reconciliation, and financial follow-up.

Solution

We designed a dashboard within Content Studio to compare budgeted hours, hours consumed, and costs associated with each production.

Hours reconciliation flow

Beyond the dashboard, we designed a reconciliation view to review hours, costs, and statuses associated with a production.

This view made it possible to move from general monitoring to operational review, distinguishing between pending, approved, and reconciled information.

Validation with stakeholders

We validated the proposal with Finance and Operations to check whether the flow covered the process's main actions:

  • Identify unreconciled hours.
  • Select pending tasks.
  • Assign each task to the corresponding budget item.
  • Update reconciliations.
  • Review the overall status from the dashboard.

The review made it possible to adjust the information hierarchy, reinforce states and visual indicators, and better connect the dashboard to daily operations.

Results

Integrated flow

The need for manual exports to external tools was eliminated.

Early deviation detection

Overuse or underuse of time and budget mismatches are identified quickly.

Structured follow-up

Issues are reviewed periodically in weekly meetings.

Simplified cost approval

Detecting and bulk-approving pending costs became easier.

The Finance team went from reviewing fragmented information across external tools to monitoring and resolving deviations from a single, integrated flow within Content Studio.
