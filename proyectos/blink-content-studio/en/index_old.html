Blink Content Studio

Content Studio is Blink.la's internal platform for managing audiovisual productions end to end. Budgets, teams, vendors, operations, and financial control.

Client Blink.la
Year 2023 - 2025
Scope Product design

Overview

Blink is an audiovisual production company that connects talent, creatives, and production teams. Content Studio is its proprietary platform: the place where project management, team members, vendors, and each production's financial control come together.

The problem

Despite having a centralized platform, several critical processes still happened outside the system or in informal conversations. This created three concrete problems:

  • Information fragmented across spreadsheets, emails, and other tools.
  • Poor visibility into statuses, costs, alerts, and approvals.
  • Heavy operational dependency on manual processes and institutional knowledge.

Processes were fragmented across the platform, spreadsheets, emails, and manual approvals. That created duplication, poor visibility, and heavy operational dependency.

My role

As Product Designer, I worked on evolving Content Studio alongside Operations, Finance, Production, and Engineering. My role was to understand complex internal processes, model them as product flows, design scalable solutions, validate with users, and ensure visual and functional consistency across the platform.

  • Discovery with internal users.
  • Mapping of operational and financial processes.
  • Design of flows, wireframes, and prototypes.
  • Validation with stakeholders and users.
  • Handoff and follow-up with engineering.
  • Evolution of the design system.

Main projects

Productions index redesign

The original view packed in too much data and made it hard to tell what needed attention. I reorganized the information, simplified the list's readability, and surfaced the statuses, alerts, and data needed to compare productions and make decisions faster.

Financial dashboard

I designed a dashboard to quickly identify the productions with the highest profit. The view consolidated key financial information to compare performance across productions, spot opportunities, and support follow-up decisions for the operations and finance teams.

Internal team booking

Internal team booking lived in a complex spreadsheet. We turned it into an integrated view inside Content Studio, where availability, assignments, workload percentages, and absences could be read at a glance.

Vendor self-onboarding

Vendor onboarding depended on spreadsheets, links, emails, and manual follow-up. I designed a guided portal for vendors to complete their information, documentation, taxes, payments, and contract signing more independently.

Design System

As Content Studio grew, I also worked on product consistency: reusable components, patterns, styles, states, and layouts. This helped speed up new features and reduce inconsistencies across modules.

Other redesigned flows

Automatic invoice generation · Expense reconciliation · Internal team financial dashboards · Vendor scoring and reviews · Vendor Portal · DAM / Asset Library · Time Tracking

The impact

More than 15 flows redesigned or integrated, less dependency on manual processes, and better operational and financial visibility for the teams who use it every day.

  • +15 flows redesigned or integrated
  • Less dependency on manual processes
  • Better operational and financial visibility
  • Greater consistency thanks to the Design System
  • UX practices embedded into the product process

Content Studio became more integrated, consistent, and operational.
