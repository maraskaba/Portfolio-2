MKR

Ad automation at scale

MKR is an AdTech platform that automates the production of display ads at high volume by editing a minimal set of variables.

Client Blink.la
Year 2025
Scope Product design

Context

MKR started as an internal innovation initiative at Blink.la to explore a new business vertical focused on automating display ads at scale.

The goal was to design a system capable of generating large volumes of assets while maintaining visual consistency, content flexibility, and creative control — all while reducing the operational load of manual production.

As Product Designer I was responsible for discovery, product design, and presentations, working alongside the Head of Design and the development team.

The problem

Manual production

Our clients' campaigns required hundreds of variants (sizes, languages, CTAs) that were produced one by one, consuming weeks of operational work with low creative value.

Market rigidity

Many existing solutions were closed SaaS platforms or expensive services, with inflexible, non-customizable workflows.

Lack of consistency

As production scaled, visual errors, inconsistencies, and review times increased.

The process

  • Researched market tools and capabilities.
  • Identified product opportunities and features.
  • Prioritized and defined the backlog together with the development team.
  • Designed workflows and editing flows.
  • Designed the main production and editing interface.
  • Defined and maintained the component library.
  • Designed features focused on automation and bulk editing.

Research & prioritization

Competitors analyzed during the research phase

Benchmark

I researched market tools and platforms with similar approaches to identify different strategies, patterns, opportunities, and limitations. I surveyed and prioritized each solution's core features.

Technology decisions

Together with the team, we explored different technical approaches (from static assets and After Effects/Lottie animations to HTML solutions with CSS and JavaScript animations). From a product and UX standpoint, I analyzed how these decisions affected editing flexibility, workflows, and the system's scalability.

Templates and design workflows

I analyzed template structures and variable logic to ensure visual consistency, content flexibility, and scalability when generating assets.

Progress status of the tool's main features

HTML ads were the better fit for the market the business was targeting.

Design challenges

Initial definitions

I worked with the development team on the initial definitions of the data model, property editing logic, layout, and the platform's overall architecture. The challenge was defining the simplest way to ship the core functionality within the MVP timeline.

Design workflow

I defined which design aspects could be turned into variables from a template and set up the workflow for a proper integration between Figma designs and MKR, making them easier to edit and scale afterward.

Editing at scale

Generating assets in bulk had to preserve consistency and flexibility. I designed automation workflows called "Collections" that let changes cascade using variables to generate multiple banners instantly. I also designed the CSV import proposal to scale ad generation.

Animations

Editing animations had to be simple to integrate. I worked with the development team on editing properties using GSAP, a JavaScript animation library for HTML. I designed a panel that organized sequences into steps, similar to what's used in presentation tools.

Integrated AI

I researched and prioritized, together with the development team, AI tools to bring into the platform. We evaluated and implemented several integrations aimed at speeding up production and prototyping workflows.

The canvas groups assets by orientation, following the logic of bulk editing.

Properties are grouped and can be edited one asset at a time or all at once.

Assets can be created in bulk by importing a CSV or linking the project to an online spreadsheet.

Export supports different formats and runs in the background.

Results

  • MKR simplifies the production process.
  • A single template can generate multiple variants while maintaining consistency.
  • Production time is significantly reduced.
